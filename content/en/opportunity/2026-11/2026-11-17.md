The caregiving burden on dementia families is real and heavy, and the idea of using AI to lower the bar for these conversations translates naturally into content topics and tool navigation pieces that plug directly into existing content production workflows.

## Stay Away From These Today 🚫

- **Strawberries and female reproductive health claims:** A narrative review backed by a single small clinical trial reporting reduced period pain — most of the evidence comes from lab and animal studies [Source](<https://www.news-medical.net/news/20261116/A-small-study-linked-daily-strawberries-to-less-period-pain-What-about-other-benefits.aspx>). Framing this as "strawberries fight aging / improve fertility" overstates early-stage evidence and carries serious compliance risk.
- **Asprosin and vascular cell senescence:** Lab research linking adipocyte-secreted asprosin to endothelial mitochondrial dysfunction and senescence — but the source explicitly notes no known intervention currently targets this process directly [Source](<https://lifespan.io/fat-cells-can-secrete-a-senescence-promoter/>). Too far from anything actionable as content or a tool.
