Demand assumption: this group consistently follows "non-pharmaceutical interventions," but willingness to pay in the Chinese market is unverified.
- **Pain points and payment rationale**: Caregivers face ongoing anxiety about "what can we do beyond medication." Health content creators need sourced topics. Both groups might pay in time cost or small fees for structured information — but payment intent is an assumption that must be validated through post-publication engagement data.
- **Deliverables**: ① A WeChat/Xiaohongshu long-form article ("Can exercise + brain training protect the brain? Here's what the research says"), with explicit study limitations noted; ② A PDF resource pack (comparison chart: aerobic exercise vs. cognitive training vs. combined, with evidence levels and source citations); ③ A Notion page aggregating similar research links from the past year.
- **What this is good for**: Content ideation, information curation
- **Minimum viable validation**: Write a Xiaohongshu or WeChat draft today on the topic "research observed that exercise + cognitive training may have a protective effect on brain regions in older adults with memory impairment," explicitly stating "research observation, not medical advice." Track engagement for 48 hours after publishing.

- **Upsell / retention path**: Free explainer → low-cost resource pack (non-pharmaceutical intervention summary list) → paid community (caregiver support + information updates) → light consulting (help organizing intervention information, no medical advice given).
- **Compliance risk**: Medium ⚠️

You can only write "a study observed changes in certain indicators among older adults with memory impairment; conclusions require validation at larger scale." Do not extend this into unclinically validated personal health judgment tools for individual users.
