## Don't Touch Today

**1. Personal Health Judgment Tools Derived from DunedinPACNI Research — Not Clinically Validated** 🚫

The DunedinPACNI study from [source](<https://www.news-medical.net/news/20261109/Could-a-single-brain-scan-reveal-how-quickly-your-body-is-aging.aspx>) is a correlational study between an MRI-derived metric and blood biomarkers (746 participants, two cohorts, observational design) — not a clinical diagnostic tool. This metric has not entered routine clinical pathways. Any direction that uses this research to provide health judgments to individual users is not supported by the current evidence stage and carries high compliance risk. The right move: add it as a research progress entry in the resource library, clearly stating the study type, sample size, and limitations.
