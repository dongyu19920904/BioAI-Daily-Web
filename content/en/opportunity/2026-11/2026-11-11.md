## Project & Tool Leads

**1. Pyrite — Protein-Ligand Pose Ranking Benchmark** 🧪

[Paper link](https://papers.cool/arxiv/2611.14511). Pyrite provides a benchmark set of 1,500 pose pairs, using the PyriteRep structured representation paired with an LLM for automated ranking. Good candidate for a tool navigation library entry, or for a piece on "what AI can and can't do for chemists." Heads up: publication date unknown, only an update timestamp; experimental stage is benchmarking, which doesn't translate to real-world drug discovery performance.

**2. Bonbon — Molecular Interaction Foundation Model** 🤖

[Paper link](https://papers.cool/arxiv/2611.14504). Bonbon was trained on roughly 3.2 trillion protein-ligand sequence tokens, with zero-shot demonstrations of three emergent capabilities: mechanism-of-action recognition, binding site localization, and covalent/non-covalent distinction. It works as a case study entry for a "AI biology foundation model progress" content series. Same caveat: publication date unknown, early research stage — do NOT write it up as "already usable for screening new drugs for age-related diseases."
