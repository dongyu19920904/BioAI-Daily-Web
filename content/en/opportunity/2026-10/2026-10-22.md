## Project and Tool Leads

**isalaberry/EpigeneticAgeCalculator** (https://github.com/isalaberry/EpigeneticAgeCalculator)

Python-implemented epigenetic age calculator based on Garma & Quintela-Fandiño (2024) model, uses Illumina EPIC v2 platform's DNA methylation β values to estimate biological age, supports multi-sample batch processing and automated report generation. Currently 1 star, early-stage project.

**What it can become:** Not suitable for direct consumer-facing tool (requires professional sequencing data, ordinary users can't provide input). Suitable for: ① Resource library entry ("Open-Source Biological Age Calculation Tools Roundup"); ② Reference case for technical popular science topics; ③ Daily report "project tracking" material — watch subsequent star growth and issue activity to judge whether research community recognizes it.
