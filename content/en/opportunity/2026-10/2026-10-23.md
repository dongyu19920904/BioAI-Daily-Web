## Stay Away Today

- **Selling raw brain scan data analysis to consumers**: Sure, public imaging datasets like KARI and IXI are accessible, but offering "brain age assessment" or "AD risk scoring" services directly to consumers requires medical diagnostics licensing, imaging equipment, privacy compliance, and liability coverage for misdiagnosis — all way out of reach for solo operators or lean teams. Stick to information curation (dataset roundups, paper breakdowns) or B2B technical consulting (helping medical institutions and research teams with data cleaning or model research). Consumer health judgment tools are off the table. 🚫
- **Psychedelic anti-aging content**: News-Medical reported on 2026-10-22 that psilocybin reshapes brain connections ([https://www.news-medical.net/news/20261022/Psilocybin-reshapes-brain-connections-through-distinct-cellular-pathways.aspx](https://www.news-medical.net/news/20261022/Psilocybin-reshapes-brain-connections-through-distinct-cellular-pathways.aspx)), but psilocybin is a controlled substance in China and restricted to clinical research in most countries. Don't frame this as an "anti-aging method" or "brain health supplement" — you'll risk illegal promotion or dangerous misinformation. You *can* write a purely observational piece on "psychedelic compounds in neurodegeneration research" with a clear disclaimer: "For research awareness only; not treatment advice." 🚫
- **Synthetic single-cell RNA data services**: The single-cell synthetic data generation method indexed on Papers.cool ([https://papers.cool/arxiv/2610.31454](https://papers.cool/arxiv/2610.31454)) is a cutting-edge research tool built for academic institutions doing privacy-preserving data sharing — not a viable "genetic data anonymization service" for solo operators or lean teams, given the bioinformatics expertise, compute resources, data security, and ethics review requirements involved. Consumer and small-business demand is also unclear. Stick to paper breakdowns or technical observation content. 🚫
