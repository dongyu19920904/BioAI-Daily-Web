## Tools & Project Leads

**isalaberry/EpigeneticAgeCalculator**

An open-source Python project based on the Garma & Quintela-Fandiño (2024) model that estimates biological age from DNA methylation β-values on the Illumina EPIC v2 platform. Supports multi-sample processing, CpG coverage analysis, missing probe handling, and automated reporting (source: [GitHub, 2026-10-13](https://github.com/isalaberry/EpigeneticAgeCalculator) — currently 1 star, early-stage project). 🔬
