### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪

Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts — all designed to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. Here's what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. But heads up — if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Our Service may use Cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse them.

- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These let the Company do things like count how many users have visited certain pages or opened emails, and they help with other website stats — like gauging how popular a section is and making sure systems and servers are running smoothly.

Cookies come in two flavors: "persistent" and "session." Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session Cookies get wiped the second you close your web browser.

Both session and persistent Cookies are used by us for the following purposes:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are critical for delivering the Service to you through the website and letting you use certain features. They help verify who you are and keep fraudulent use of user accounts at bay. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for can't be provided — we only use them to get those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice of Cookie Acceptance**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us recognize whether you've accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website — like your login info or language preferences. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you drop by.

Our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy is where you'll find more info about the Cookies we use and your options when it comes to Cookies.
