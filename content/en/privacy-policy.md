### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to track activity on our service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts designed to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our service. Here's what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file dropped on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all cookies or to prompt you before sending one. If you don't accept cookies, though, some parts of our service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse cookies, our service may use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These let the Company count how many people have visited certain pages or opened certain emails, and they help with other site stats (like tracking how popular a section is and checking that systems and servers are working properly).

Cookies come in two flavors: "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies stick around on your computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while session cookies get wiped the moment you close your browser.

We use both session and persistent cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are crucial for delivering the service through the website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify who you are and stop fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these cookies, the service you asked for can't be provided, and we only use them to deliver that service to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Cookie Notice Acceptance**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies help us recognize whether you've accepted the use of cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website, like your login info or language preference. The goal is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you drop by.

More info on the cookies we use and your choices around cookies can be found in our Cookies Policy or the cookies section of our privacy policy.
