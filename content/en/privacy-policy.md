### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪

Cookies and similar tracking tech help us keep tabs on activity across our Service and stash certain info. We use beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track information, plus improve and analyze our Service. Here's what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up before sending one. But heads up — if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser to block Cookies, our Service may use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Parts of our Service and our emails might include small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These let us count how many people have visited certain pages or opened our emails, and help with other website stats (like tracking how popular a section is and making sure our systems and servers are running smoothly).

Cookies come in two flavors: "persistent" and "session." Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or device even when you go offline, while session Cookies get wiped the second you close your browser.

Here's what we use session and persistent Cookies for:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These are the must-haves — they keep the Service running and let you use key features. They help verify who you are and keep fraudsters out of your account. Without these, the Service you asked for simply can't be delivered. We only use them to provide that Service to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies remember whether you've given the thumbs-up to using Cookies on our site.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These let us remember your choices — like your login info or language preference — so you get a more personalized experience without having to re-enter your prefs every single time you visit.

Want to dig deeper into our Cookies and your choices? Check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy for the full scoop.
