### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts that collect and track info, and help us improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to prompt you when a cookie is being sent. But heads up — if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service may use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These let the Company count, for example, users who have visited those pages or opened emails, and they're used for other related website stats (like logging how popular a section is and verifying the integrity of systems and servers).

Cookies can be either "persistent" or "session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session Cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser.

We use both session and persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for delivering the Service to you through the website and letting you use certain features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to provide those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to identify whether you've accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website, like remembering your login details or language preferences. The point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you use the site.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our privacy policy.
