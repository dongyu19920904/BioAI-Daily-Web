### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🔍
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to track activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to prompt you when a Cookie is being sent. But heads-up — if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service may use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These allow the Company to, for example, count how many people have visited those pages or opened those emails, and they're used for other related website statistics (like gauging how popular a section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "persistent" or "session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session Cookies get deleted the moment you close your web browser.

We use both session and persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are crucial for providing you with available services through the website and letting you use certain features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for can't be provided, and we only use them to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice of Cookie Acceptance**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to recognize whether you've already accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website, like remembering your login info or language preference. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you use the website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our privacy policy.
