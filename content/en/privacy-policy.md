Usage data gets collected automatically when you use the service.

When you access the service via a mobile device, we might automatically collect certain info, including but not limited to the type of mobile device you're using, your mobile device's unique ID, your mobile device's IP address, your mobile operating system, the type of mobile internet browser you're using, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

When you visit our service or access it through a mobile device, we might also collect info your browser sends our way.

### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to track activity on our service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our service. Technologies we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all cookies or to prompt you when a cookie's being sent. But heads up — if you don't accept cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our service. Our service may use cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) that allow the Company to, for example, count how many users visited those pages or opened emails, and are used for other related website stats (like recording how popular a certain section is and verifying the integrity of systems and servers).

Cookies can be either "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while session cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser.

We use both session and persistent cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are crucial for providing the service to you through the website and letting you use certain features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these cookies, the service you asked for can't be provided, and we only use these cookies to give you that service.

- **Cookie Policy / Notice of Cookie Acceptance**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are used to identify whether you've accepted the use of cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website, like remembering your login info or language preferences. The point of these cookies is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you use the site.

For more info about the cookies we use and your choices regarding cookies, check out our Cookie Policy or the cookies section of our privacy policy.
