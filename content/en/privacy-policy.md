### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all Cookies or to prompt you when a Cookie is sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you may not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service may use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) that allow the Company to, for example, count how many users have visited those pages or opened emails, and are used for other related website statistics (such as recording the popularity of a section and verifying the integrity of systems and servers).

Cookies can be either "persistent" or "session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session Cookies are deleted the moment you close your web browser.

We use both session and persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for providing you with available services through the website and enabling you to use certain features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've requested can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to provide those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to identify whether users have accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies allow us to remember the choices you make while using the website, such as remembering your login info or language preferences. The purpose of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you use the website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, please visit our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
