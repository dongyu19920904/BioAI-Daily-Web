### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies used include beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. Technologies we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to prompt you when a Cookie is being sent. But heads up — if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject Cookies, our Service may use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs) that let us, for example, count how many users visited those pages or opened an email, and they're used for other related site stats (like logging how popular a section is and verifying the integrity of systems and servers).

Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session Cookies get deleted the moment you close your web browser.

We use both session and persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for delivering the Service to you through the website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, we can't provide the services you've asked for — we only use them to give you access to those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to identify whether you've accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website, like your login info or language preferences. The goal here is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you swing by.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
