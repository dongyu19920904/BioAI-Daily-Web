### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can set your browser to refuse all Cookies or to prompt you when a Cookie gets sent. That said, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service may use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, plus gather other related website stats (like recording how popular a section is and checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies get deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both session and persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are essential for giving you the services available through the Website and enabling you to use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies identify whether users have accepted the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when using the Website, like remembering your login details or language preference. Their purpose is to give you a more personalized experience and help you avoid having to re-enter your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
