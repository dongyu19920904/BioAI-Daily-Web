### Tracking Technologies and Cookies

Cookies and similar tracking technologies — we use them to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts designed to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our Service. Here's what might be in the mix:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies** — a Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to ping you when a Cookie is being sent. But heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Our Service may use Cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse them.

- **Web Beacons** — certain parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These let the Company, among other things, count how many users have visited certain pages or opened an email, and they're used for other related website stats (like gauging how popular a section is and verifying the integrity of our system and servers).

Cookies come in two flavors: "persistent" or "session." Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session Cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser.

Both session and persistent Cookies are used by us for the following purposes:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are crucial for delivering the services available through the website and letting you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for can't be provided — and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookie Policy / Cookie Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us recognize whether you've accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website, like your login info or language preferences. The idea is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your prefs every single time you drop by.

For more details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, check out our Cookie Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
