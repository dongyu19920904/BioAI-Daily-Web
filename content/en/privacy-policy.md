### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track information, and to improve and analyze our Service. Technologies we use may include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to prompt you when a cookie is sent. But heads up — if you don't accept cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Our Service may use cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse them.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These let the Company count, for instance, how many users visited those pages or opened their emails, and they're used for other related website statistics (like recording how popular a section is and verifying the integrity of systems and servers).

Cookies can be either "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser.

We use both session and persistent cookies for the following purposes:

- **Essential/Basic Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are absolutely critical for providing the Service through the website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify user identity and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are used to figure out whether you've accepted the use of cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website, like remembering your login info or language preferences. The point of these cookies is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the website.

Want more info about the cookies we use and your cookie choices? Check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our privacy policy.
