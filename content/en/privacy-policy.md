### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to track activity on our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we deploy includes beacons, tags, and scripts — all aimed at collecting and tracking info, and improving and analyzing our Service. Here's what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. But here's the catch — if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to refuse Cookies, our Service may use them.

- **Web Beacons**: Certain parts of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These let companies, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened emails, and they're used for other related website stats (like recording how popular a section is and checking that systems and servers are running as they should).

Cookies come in two flavors: "persistent" or "session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while session Cookies get wiped the moment you close your web browser.

Here's what we use session and persistent Cookies for:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential for delivering the Service through the website and letting you use certain features. They help verify who you are and keep fraudulent use of user accounts at bay. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for can't be provided — and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice of Acceptance of Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies help us figure out whether you've accepted the use of Cookies on the website.

- **Functional Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Manager**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make while using the website — like your login info or language preferences. The goal is to give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the website.

For more details on the Cookies we use and your choices around Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
