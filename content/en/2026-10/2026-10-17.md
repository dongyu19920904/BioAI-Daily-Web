### 2. Blocking the STING Pathway Preserves Bone Density in Aging Mice 🦴

[Lifespan.io reports](https://lifespan.io/blocking-sting-helps-preserve-bone-in-aging-mice/) that a Chinese research team published in *Advanced Science* showing that macrophage-specific silencing of *Sting* in 12–18-month-old mice significantly preserved bone density, bone structure, and reduced bone marrow adipocyte numbers. The study mapped out a signaling chain: bone marrow adipocyte-secreted **DPP4** activates STING via TLR4 in concert with mitochondrial DNA leakage, which then drives **CD38**-mediated extracellular NAD⁺ degradation and impairs mitochondrial function in neighboring bone progenitor cells. The drug combo (STING inhibitor H-151 + NMN) performed comparably to the established anti-osteoporosis drug alendronate. Caveats: mouse study, small sample, and the combined treatment makes it hard to isolate individual contributions — no human data yet.

### 3. Monte Rosa's NEK7 Degrader Shows Drops Across Multiple Inflammation Markers in Phase 1 📉

[Longevity.Technology reports](https://longevity.technology/news/heart-pill-cuts-inflammation-markers-in-early-trial/) that **Monte Rosa Therapeutics** completed the GFORCE-1 study (Phase 1/pilot, 4-week dosing) in 108 obese subjects at elevated cardiovascular risk. The molecular glue degrader MRT-8102 drove a median **~80–90%** drop in NEK7 protein levels; downstream markers followed — hsCRP down **85%**, IL-6 down **54%**; plaque-associated signals calprotectin, S100A12, and SAA fell by **56%, 46%, and 51%** respectively. The adverse event rate (33%) was close to placebo (30%), with no increased infection signal. Data came from a company announcement — no peer-reviewed publication yet. A 4-week improvement in blood biomarkers does not equal fewer cardiac events; Phase 2b GFORCE-2 is expected to kick off in H1 2027.

### 9. Senescent Cell-Derived Extracellular Vesicles Spread Aging Signals Independently of Classic SASP 🔬

[Lifespan.io reports](https://lifespan.io/untangling-differences-in-senescent-cellular-signaling/) that a cell study published in *Aging* compared the secretomes of H-RASG12V-induced senescent cells versus p16-overexpressing cells: p16 cells barely secreted soluble SASP factors like IL-1β and IL-6, but pumped out more small extracellular vesicles (sEVs). Those sEVs drove recipient cells to express p16 and SA-β-gal (senescence markers) and triggered DNA damage (γH2AX) — without triggering classic inflammatory cytokines. The effect was validated using sEVs from elderly donors applied to infant fibroblasts. Caveats: in vitro only, the specific cargo inside the sEVs hasn't been identified, and whether targeting sEVs is therapeutically feasible hasn't been explored.

## **🧪 Frontier Research**

### Bisphenol Mixtures Reprogram Epigenetic State in Human Breast Organoids

According to [GEN's reporting](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/cancer/exposure-to-common-endocrine-disrupting-chemicals-may-alter-normal-breast-development/), researchers from **Tufts University and Boston University** published in *PNAS* using 3D breast organoids from **13** healthy young women to test individual and combined exposures to BPA, BPS, and BPF. Single exposures showed limited effects, but low-dose mixture exposure — at concentrations matching real human blood and urine detection levels — disrupted normal breast development in most donors' organoids, inducing extracellular matrix remodeling, epithelial plasticity, and DNA methylation reprogramming. The molecular alteration patterns closely overlapped with data from invasive lobular carcinoma patients. An AI-assisted high-content screening platform was used to quantify developmental disruption. Caveats: in vitro organoid model, small donor sample size, cannot prove the chemicals cause cancer, and whether these molecular changes persist in human tissue and lead to disease requires prospective study.

## **⌘ Top Open Source Projects**

### kkontras/NeuroAtlas: Cross-Task Benchmark Suite for EEG Foundation Models

[NeuroAtlas](https://github.com/kkontras/NeuroAtlas) is an EEG foundation model evaluation framework covering four task categories: sleep staging, **brain age** estimation, epilepsy detection, and brain-computer interfaces (BCI). The paper was accepted at NeurIPS 2026. It's a solid pick for developers and researchers working on neural signal processing, brain age biomarkers, and cross-task transfer learning. The repo dropped on 2026-10-16 with **18 stars** currently; check the repo README for license details, dataset terms, and environment requirements — none of this has been independently verified.

### JackieHanLab/PAOPAC: Proteome-Driven Organ Proxy Aging Clock

[PAOPAC](https://github.com/JackieHanLab/PAOPAC) (Proteome-Aware Organ Proxy Aging Clock) uses proteomic data to build biological age proxy clocks for individual organs — a multi-omics aging measurement tool. It's aimed at researchers working on proteomics and aging biology or organ-specific biological age estimation. The repo was published on 2026-09-21 with **2 stars**; check the repo documentation for the specific datasets used, license, and algorithm details — all pending independent verification.
