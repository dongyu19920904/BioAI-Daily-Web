### 2. Blocking the STING Pathway Protects Bone Density in Aged Mice 🦴

According to [Lifespan.io's coverage](https://lifespan.io/blocking-sting-helps-preserve-bone-in-aging-mice/), a Chinese research team published in *Advanced Science* showing that macrophage-specific silencing of the *Sting* gene in 12–18-month-old mice significantly preserved bone density and structure while reducing bone marrow adipocyte numbers. The study also mapped out a signaling chain: bone marrow adipocytes secrete **DPP4**, which cooperates with TLR4 and mitochondrial DNA leakage to activate STING, which then drives **CD38** to degrade extracellular NAD⁺ and impair mitochondrial function in neighboring bone progenitor cells. The drug combo (STING inhibitor H-151 + NMN) performed comparably to the established anti-osteoporosis drug alendronate. Caveats: mouse study, small sample, combo design makes it hard to isolate each drug's contribution, no human data yet.

### 3. Monte Rosa's NEK7 Degrader Posts Multi-Marker Inflammation Drops in Phase 1 🔬

According to [Longevity.Technology's coverage](https://longevity.technology/news/heart-pill-cuts-inflammation-markers-in-early-trial/), Monte Rosa Therapeutics ran GFORCE-1 (Phase 1/pilot, 4-week dosing) in 108 obese, high-cardiovascular-risk subjects. The molecular glue degrader MRT-8102 drove a median **~80–90%** drop in NEK7 protein levels; downstream markers followed — hsCRP down **85%**, IL-6 down **54%**, and plaque-associated signals calprotectin, S100A12, and SAA down **56%, 46%, and 51%** respectively. Adverse event rates (33%) were close to placebo (30%), with no increase in infection risk. Data came from a company press release — no peer-reviewed publication yet. A 4-week biomarker win ≠ reduced cardiac events; the follow-on Phase 2b GFORCE-2 is expected to kick off in H1 2027.

### 4. Prenuvo Opens 200K Whole-Body MRI Scans to 50+ Research Institutions 📊

### 9. Senescent Cell-Derived Extracellular Vesicles Spread Aging Signals Independently of Classic SASP 🔬

According to [Lifespan.io's coverage](https://lifespan.io/untangling-differences-in-senescent-cellular-signaling/), a cell study published in *Aging* compared the secretomes of H-RASG12V-induced senescent cells versus p16-overexpressing cells: p16 cells barely secreted soluble SASP factors like IL-1β or IL-6, but pumped out more small extracellular vesicles (sEVs). Those sEVs drove recipient cells to express p16 and SA-β-gal (senescence markers) and induced DNA damage (γH2AX) — without triggering classic inflammatory cytokine secretion. The effect was validated using sEVs from elderly donors applied to infant fibroblasts. Caveats: in vitro only, specific sEV cargo unidentified, targeting sEVs therapeutically remains unexplored.

## **🧪 Cutting-Edge Research**

### Bisphenol Mixtures Reprogram Epigenetic State in Human Breast Organoids ⚗️

According to [GEN's coverage](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/cancer/exposure-to-common-endocrine-disrupting-chemicals-may-alter-normal-breast-development/), researchers from Tufts University and Boston University published in *PNAS* using 3D breast organoids from **13** healthy young women to test the effects of BPA, BPS, and BPF — individually and as a mixture. Solo exposures showed limited changes, but low-dose mixed exposure (matching concentrations actually detected in human blood and urine) disrupted normal breast development in most donors' organoids, inducing extracellular matrix remodeling, epithelial plasticity, and DNA methylation reprogramming. The molecular change patterns closely overlapped with data from invasive lobular carcinoma patients. An AI-assisted high-content screening platform was used to quantify developmental disruption. Caveats: in vitro organoid model, small donor sample, can't prove chemicals cause cancer, and whether these molecular changes persist in human tissue and lead to disease still needs prospective study.

## **⌘ Top Open-Source Projects**

### kkontras/NeuroAtlas: EEG Foundation Model Cross-Task Benchmark Suite 🧠

[NeuroAtlas](https://github.com/kkontras/NeuroAtlas) is an EEG foundation model evaluation framework covering four task categories: sleep staging, **brain age** estimation, epilepsy detection, and brain-computer interfaces (BCI). The paper was accepted at NeurIPS 2026. Great fit for developers and researchers working on neural signal processing, brain age biomarkers, and cross-task transfer learning. Repo published 2026-10-16, currently at **18 stars**; check the repo README for license details, dataset terms, and environment requirements — not independently verified.

### JackieHanLab/PAOPAC: Proteome-Driven Organ Proxy Aging Clock ⏱️

[PAOPAC](https://github.com/JackieHanLab/PAOPAC) (Proteome-Aware Organ Proxy Aging Clock) uses proteomics data to build biological age proxy clocks for individual organs — a multi-omics aging measurement tool. Good fit for researchers working on proteomics and aging biology or organ-specific biological age estimation. Repo published 2026-09-21, **2 stars**; datasets used, license, and algorithmic details are all in the repo — none independently verified.
