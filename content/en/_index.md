### 2. Blocking STING Preserves Bone Density in Aging Mice 🦴

Per [Lifespan.io's reporting](https://lifespan.io/blocking-sting-helps-preserve-bone-in-aging-mice/), a Chinese research team published in *Advanced Science* showing that macrophage-specific *Sting* gene silencing in 12–18 month-old mice significantly preserved bone density, bone structure, and reduced bone marrow adipocyte numbers. The team also mapped a signaling chain: bone marrow adipocytes secrete **DPP4**, which cooperates with mitochondrial DNA leakage via TLR4 to activate STING, which then drives **CD38** to degrade extracellular NAD⁺ and impair nearby bone progenitor cell mitochondrial function. The drug combo (STING inhibitor H-151 + NMN) performed on par with the established osteoporosis drug alendronate. Caveats: mouse study, small sample, and the combined treatment makes it hard to isolate each drug's contribution — no human data yet.

### 3. Monte Rosa's NEK7 Degrader Shows Multi-Marker Inflammation Drop in Phase 1 Data 📉

Per [Longevity.Technology's reporting](https://longevity.technology/news/heart-pill-cuts-inflammation-markers-in-early-trial/), Monte Rosa Therapeutics completed the GFORCE-1 study (Phase 1/pilot, 4-week dosing) in 108 obese subjects with high cardiovascular risk. Their molecular glue degrader MRT-8102 drove a median **80–90%** drop in NEK7 protein levels; downstream markers followed — hsCRP dropped **85%**, IL-6 dropped **54%**; plaque-related signals calprotectin, S100A12, and SAA fell **56%, 46%, and 51%** respectively. The adverse event rate (33%) was close to placebo (30%), with no increased infection risk. Data came from a company press release, not a peer-reviewed paper — and a 4-week biomarker improvement doesn't equal reduced cardiac events. Phase 2b GFORCE-2 is expected to kick off in H1 2027.

### 9. Senescent Cell Extracellular Vesicles Drive Aging Independently — No Classic SASP Required

Per [Lifespan.io's reporting](https://lifespan.io/untangling-differences-in-senescent-cellular-signaling/), a cell study in *Aging* compared the secretomes of H-RASG12V-induced senescent cells vs. p16-overexpressing cells: p16 cells barely secreted soluble SASP factors like IL-1β and IL-6, but pumped out significantly more small extracellular vesicles (sEVs). These sEVs drove recipient cells to express p16 and SA-β-gal (senescence markers) and induced DNA damage (γH2AX) — without triggering classic inflammatory cytokine secretion. The team validated this using sEVs from elderly donors applied to infant fibroblasts. Caveats: in vitro only, the specific cargo inside these sEVs hasn't been identified, and whether targeting sEVs is therapeutically viable is still an open question. 🔬

## **🧪 Frontier Research**

### Bisphenol Mixtures Reprogram Epigenetic State in Human Breast Organoids

Per [GEN's reporting](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/cancer/exposure-to-common-endocrine-disrupting-chemicals-may-alter-normal-breast-development/), researchers from Tufts University and Boston University published in *PNAS* using 3D breast organoids from **13** healthy young women to test BPA, BPS, and BPF individually and in combination. Single-compound exposures had limited effects — but low-dose mixture exposure (at concentrations matching actual human blood and urine levels) disrupted normal breast development in most donors' organoids, inducing extracellular matrix remodeling, epithelial plasticity, and DNA methylation reprogramming. The molecular alteration patterns closely overlapped with invasive lobular carcinoma patient data. The team used an AI-assisted high-content screening platform to quantify developmental disruption. Caveats: in vitro organoid model, small donor cohort — this doesn't prove the chemicals cause cancer, and whether these molecular changes persist in human tissue and lead to disease still needs prospective validation. 🧬

### kkontras/NeuroAtlas: Cross-Task Benchmark Suite for EEG Foundation Models

[NeuroAtlas](https://github.com/kkontras/NeuroAtlas) is an EEG foundation model evaluation framework covering four task categories: sleep staging, **brain age** estimation, epilepsy detection, and brain-computer interface (BCI). The paper was accepted at NeurIPS 2026. Great fit for developers and researchers working on neural signal processing, brain age biomarkers, and cross-task transfer learning. The repo dropped on 2026-10-16 with **18 stars** — check the README for license details, dataset usage terms, and environment requirements, all pending independent verification.

### JackieHanLab/PAOPAC: Proteome-Driven Organ Proxy Aging Clock

[PAOPAC](https://github.com/JackieHanLab/PAOPAC) (Proteome-Aware Organ Proxy Aging Clock) uses proteomic data to build biological age proxy clocks for individual organs — a multi-omics aging measurement tool. Solid fit for researchers working on proteomics, aging biology, and organ-specific biological age estimation. The repo was published 2026-09-21 with **2 stars** — check the repo documentation for the specific datasets used, license, and algorithm details, all pending independent verification.
